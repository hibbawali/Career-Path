# Career-Path
Pakistan Scholarship Finder. Find Your Scholarship - Build Your Career.<br>

CareerPath is a student-focused platform designed to help students discover scholarships, educational opportunities, and career guidance in one place. Many students struggle to find relevant opportunities because information is scattered across multiple websites, deadlines are difficult to track, and eligibility requirements are often confusing. CareerPath aims to simplify this process by providing a centralized and user-friendly solution.

## Problem Statement

Students often spend a lot of time searching for scholarships and career opportunities online. They may miss important deadlines, apply for opportunities they are not eligible for, or fail to find programs that match their academic profile. This creates confusion and limits access to valuable opportunities.

## Solution

CareerPath allows students to enter information such as their degree level, CGPA, and location. Based on these details, the platform displays relevant scholarships and opportunities that match their profile. This helps students quickly identify suitable options without spending hours searching different websites.

## Key Features

### Scholarship Discovery

Students can browse and search scholarships available for different degree levels and academic backgrounds.

### Profile-Based Filtering

The platform filters opportunities according to the student's degree, CGPA, and location, making the results more relevant.

### Opportunity Cards

Each opportunity is displayed in a clean card layout showing important details such as eligibility criteria, deadlines, and application links.

### Deadline Tracking

Upcoming deadlines are highlighted so students can easily identify urgent opportunities and avoid missing important dates.

### User-Friendly Interface

The website is designed with a simple and responsive interface that is easy to use on different devices.

## Development Process

The project was developed using Kiro IDE. Scholarship data was collected and organized into a structured JSON format. The website was then built to read this data and provide filtering functionality based on user inputs. Kiro assisted in generating code, improving development speed, and organizing project components.
